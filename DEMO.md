# VAi Demonstration Plan

**Planned synthetic walkthrough. No public runnable demo or recording currently exists.**

## Scenario and evidence boundary

Use a fictional healthcare technology company called **Northstar Health Analytics**. The name is an illustrative scenario label, not a reference to any real organization. Use fictional people, invented company details, and a reviewed synthetic dataset.

Research responses and source documents will be mocked fixtures, clearly labeled as simulated. A fixture-backed fact illustrates evidence handling; it is not a verified real-world fact or evidence that live research completed successfully. The demonstration must use an isolated environment with live providers, connectors, and scheduler dispatch disabled.

## Three-to-five-minute sequence

| Time | Interaction | Product point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:30 | Begin at VAi's conversational workspace and request research on the fictional company | One place to begin work |
| 0:30–1:20 | Inspect the simulated company record and its fixture documents | Distinguish source material, approved example context, inference, and evidence gaps |
| 1:20–2:30 | Open a synthetic ROI/TCO scenario and change one assumption | Financial figures come from the deterministic engine |
| 2:30–3:35 | Prepare a follow-up proposal and inspect its review or assignment state | Assistance and authorization are distinct |
| 3:35–4:10 | Optionally inspect a synthetic Signal finding | Monitoring can feed reviewable work |
| 4:10–4:30 | Close by identifying what was simulated and what remains unfinished | Make the evidence boundary explicit |

### Research interaction

Example request: “Research the fictional company Northstar Health Analytics using the supplied demonstration fixtures and explain its potential fit with our example strategy.”

Show the basis of the response, including gaps or uncertainty. All strategy context must also be synthetic. Do not imply that a live provider retrieved the fictional company.

### Deterministic analysis

Use synthetic financial inputs and label their units and assumptions. If the isolated demonstration can run the actual calculation engine, show an input change and the recalculated result. If only prerecorded or mocked outputs are available, label that segment accordingly and do not call it a live engine demonstration.

### Reviewed work

Show a proposal remaining pending until an appropriately permissioned fictional user acts. Assignment and approval are different behaviors: narrate only the state transitions actually demonstrated. No external message or action should execute.

## Preparation still required

- A reviewed synthetic dataset and suitable isolated demonstration environment.
- A rehearsed route through the screens, with no internal business records or active live integrations.
- Inspection of all visible identities, source links, account details, and browser content.
- Verification of the depicted interactions, followed by review of the final screenshots or recording.

The existing browser acceptance harness is not yet a reviewed portfolio demonstration mode. This plan does not claim that the capture prerequisites have been completed.

## Closing statement

“This walkthrough uses synthetic data and simulated research to illustrate VAi's workflow design. The internal implementation, selected live integrations, and production deployment have different evidence and readiness requirements.”
